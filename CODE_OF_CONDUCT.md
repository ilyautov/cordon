# Code of conduct

This is a small project about a narrow, adversarial subject. The rules are short because there are few of them, not because they are decorative.

## What is expected

**Argue with the claim, not the person.** "This check misses a fragmented tag sequence, here is the input" is the whole genre. It stays that genre no matter how wrong the other side turns out to be.

**Be exact about attacks.** A vulnerability report is welcome; a working exploit posted in a public issue before there is a fix is not, however good the intent. The private channel is in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

**Do not carry the subject matter into the conduct.** This project is about text that pretends to be an instruction. Writing to a maintainer or a contributor in a way meant to manipulate them rather than inform them, including through an agent, is treated as what it is.

## What gets a reply and then a block

Personal attacks, harassment, unwanted contact after being asked to stop, demeaning remarks about who somebody is rather than what they wrote, deliberately reposting somebody's private details. Also: repeatedly reopening a settled decision in order to wear people down.

Sustained rudeness towards someone giving free help costs everyone here more than the contribution is worth.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and code review in this repository, plus private messages that begin here.

## Enforcement

Report it privately: the **Security** tab, **Report a vulnerability**, which is a private channel and works for this too. Say what happened and link to it. What follows is one of: a note asking for a change of tone, a temporary block, or a permanent one. Whoever reports something is not told to work it out themselves, and the report is not passed on to the person it is about with the reporter's name attached unless they agree.

The maintainer decides, and the maintainer is also bound by all of the above. If the problem is the maintainer's own behaviour, say so publicly in an issue; there is no higher authority here to appeal to, and the record being public is the only real check.
